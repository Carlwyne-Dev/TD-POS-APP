Running a sari-sari store or small retail business can be chaotic—manual inventory tracking, slow sales recording, and managing customer utang can quickly become overwhelming.

TindaDone simplifies daily store operations with a fast, modern, offline-first store management app built for sari-sari stores, boutiques, and local businesses.

With TindaDone, you can:
✅ Record sales quickly
✅ Track inventory in real time
✅ Manage customer utang
✅ Monitor expenses
✅ View daily sales reports
✅ Keep your store organized—all in one app

Simple, reliable, and built to work even without internet.
