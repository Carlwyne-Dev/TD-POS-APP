# sPOSify 🏪

> **A fast, modern, and offline-first store management tool built for sari-sari stores, boutiques, and local businesses.**

Running a small retail business can be chaotic. Between manual inventory tracking, slow sales recording, and managing customer *utang* (credit), things can quickly become overwhelming. 

**sPOSify** simplifies your daily store operations. It provides a reliable, professional-grade management experience that works entirely offline, helping store owners focus less on manual tracking and more on growing their business.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 🛒 Quick Sales Recording
Built for fast-paced daily transactions without the complexity of traditional enterprise systems.
- Lightning-fast cart system for quick tallying
- Add products instantly through search or shortcuts
- Adjust quantities with simple + / − controls
- Real-time cart totals
- Supports both Cash and GCash payment records
- Sell by piece or by pack with Bulk Mode support

### 📦 Real-Time Inventory Tracking
Track stock with precision and ease, preventing stockouts.
- Full product catalog with visual product cards
- Product photos via camera or gallery
- Automatic stock deduction per sale
- Restock logging with cost tracking
- Low-stock threshold alerts

### 📓 Customer Utang (Credit) Management
Built specifically for local business workflows.
- Customer credit records and outstanding balance tracking
- Direct checkout to *Utang*
- Transaction notes and partial payment logging
- Full payment settlement

### 💰 Profit & Expense Monitoring
Understand not just revenue—but actual profit.
- Profit per item based on Cost Price vs. Selling Price
- Track store expenses (Utility Bills, Supplier Purchases, etc.)
- Clearer profit visibility at the end of the day

### 📊 Daily Sales Reports & Analytics
Understand how your business performs daily.
- Daily Sales and Revenue Breakdowns
- Cash vs. GCash Reports
- Transaction Count & Average Sale
- Best-Selling Products
- Inventory Value and Outstanding Credit

### ☁️ Offline-First Reliability
sPOSify is built for real-world business conditions. Even with unstable internet, core operations remain fully functional:
✅ Recording sales works
✅ Inventory updates work
✅ Credit logging works

### ⚠️ Disclaimer
**sPOSify is a store management and internal record-keeping tool designed for sari-sari stores and small businesses. It helps users manage sales, inventory, *utang*, expenses, and reports for daily operations.**

**sPOSify currently does not issue official BIR receipts and is not a substitute for BIR-accredited POS systems where legally required.**
